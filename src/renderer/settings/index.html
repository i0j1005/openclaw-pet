Behavior
Chat, reactions, and motion
          

        

          
ReactionsChange the look after a reply: happy, praise, encourage, shy, sad, tired, error or question. Decided locally from the reply text, never with an extra prompt.

          
        

        

          
Hover chatShow the quick chat bar when the mouse is over the character. Clicking always works.

          
        

        

          
Speech bubbleAfter you press Enter the input turns into a bubble with your message and the reply. Use its header buttons to copy, collapse, or close it; drag the corner to resize.

          

            
          

        

        
Ambient motion — looping motion per state. Off by default: it keeps the GPU a little busy.

        

        
Reaction length — how long each reaction stays before the character goes back to idle.
